# The Deep End

A website that takes teens on a dive from the ocean surface to the bottom of the
Mariana Trench, explaining why earth and ocean science matter along the way.

## What's in here

| File         | What it does                                                        |
|--------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `index.html` | All the words and the page structure. Edit this to change content.  |
| `styles.css` | Colors, fonts, and layout. The colors are listed at the very top.   |
| `script.js`  | The interactive parts: depth gauge, flashlight, quizzes, facts.     |

No build tools or installs are needed. It's plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## See it on your computer

Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser. After you edit a file,
save it and refresh the page.

## Easy things to change

- **Add your name:** search `index.html` for `Made by a high school student`
  and put your name in that sentence.
- **Add a "Spread the word" fact:** in `script.js`, find `const FACTS = [` and
  add a new line in quotes, ending with a comma.
- **Change a quiz question:** in `script.js`, find `const QUIZ = [`. Each
  answer is tagged with the career path it counts toward.
- **Add a timeline event in New York:** in `index.html`, copy one
  `<li class="tl-item">...</li>` block in the twilight zone and edit it.
- **Change colors:** edit the values at the top of `styles.css` under `:root`.

If you add a fact, add its source to the Sources list at the bottom of
`index.html` too. Credibility matters when you're trying to convince people.

## Put it online for free (GitHub Pages)

1. Make a free account at github.com.
2. Click **New repository**. Name it something like `the-deep-end`, set it to
   Public, and create it.
3. Click **Add file → Upload files** and drag in `index.html`, `styles.css`,
   and `script.js`. Click **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under "Branch," pick `main` and `/ (root)`, then
   click **Save**.
5. Wait a minute or two. Your site will be live at
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/the-deep-end/`.

To update the site later, upload the changed files again.
